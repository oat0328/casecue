## 1. MISSION & GUIDING PRINCIPLES

CaseCue is an AI-powered assistant designed to help educators organize information, review records, draft documentation, prepare meetings, and save time.

CaseCue does not replace educators, related service providers, administrators, evaluators, or IEP teams.

**The Final CaseCue Principle (applies everywhere, no exceptions):**

- CaseCue assists educators. CaseCue does not replace educators.
- CaseCue supports professional judgement. CaseCue does not make decisions.
- All educational decisions, placement decisions, eligibility determinations, service recommendations, behavioral determinations, accommodations, goals, amendments, and compliance decisions must be reviewed and approved by qualified educational professionals.
- All AI-generated content must be reviewed and approved by qualified educational professionals before use.

**Branding & content rules (enforced platform-wide):**

- Spelling is always "CaseCue" (matches getcasecue.com).
- Never label AI output as legal advice, compliance-certified, or FERPA-certified. Use "potential issue" or "educator review required."
- Never invent student facts — AI uses only verified context from uploaded documents and the student record, or states that information is missing.
- All AI evidence references point back to the source document/record.
- Contact address on public-facing pages: contact@getcasecue.com.
- No third-party builder branding in the production app.
- Never advertise a feature that does not exist (marketing-claims audit rule).

## 2. HUMAN REVIEW DISCLAIMER SYSTEM

Disclaimers appear on **every AI-generated output**. No AI output is ever presented as final documentation.

### 2.1 Global AI Disclaimer (all AI-generated content)

> ⚠ **AI-GENERATED DRAFT FOR HUMAN REVIEW**
>
> This content was generated by CaseCue using uploaded documents and available student information.
> The content is intended to assist educators and should not be considered final documentation.
> All information must be reviewed, edited, verified, and approved by qualified educational professionals before use.
> CaseCue does not make educational decisions, eligibility determinations, placement recommendations, behavioral determinations, or compliance decisions.
> Final responsibility remains with the educational team.

### 2.2 Section-specific disclaimers

| Output | Disclaimer (displayed with the output) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Every IEP section | **DRAFT IEP LANGUAGE** — Generated using uploaded records and available data. Review for: Accuracy, Completeness, Compliance, District Requirements, Team Decisions. Do not finalize without team review. | ✅ |
| Present Levels | Suggested Present Levels generated by AI. Review all assessment data, progress data, observations, and educational records before inclusion in official IEP documents. | ✅ |
| Goals | Suggested Annual Goals generated by AI. Review baselines, measurement methods, mastery criteria, and progress reporting schedules. Goals must be reviewed and approved by the educational team. | ✅ |
| Accommodations | Suggested accommodations generated from uploaded records. Review for appropriateness, effectiveness, and individual student needs before implementation. | ✅ |
| SDI | Suggested Specially Designed Instruction generated by AI. Staff must verify recommendations align with student needs, district practices, and applicable regulations. | ✅ |
| Amendments | Draft amendment language generated by AI. Review all amendment language and ensure compliance with local district procedures before use. | ✅ |
| BIP | Behavior support recommendations generated by AI. Professional review required before implementing behavior interventions. Behavior plans must be developed and approved per district procedures and student needs. | ✅ |
| FBA | AI-generated analysis supports review of existing behavioral information. CaseCue does not conduct evaluations or determine behavioral function. Qualified personnel must review all findings. | ✅ |
| Meeting Script | Meeting scripts are for presentation support only. Case managers must verify all content before use in meetings. The script does not replace professional judgement or official documentation. | ✅ |
| Meeting Cheat Sheet / Navigator | This summary is a meeting support tool. Review against the current IEP and supporting documentation before presentation. | ✅ |

All section disclaimers are delivered by one standardized, shared component (`AiDisclaimer`) so wording is identical everywhere.

### 2.3 AI confidence warnings (all extracted data)

Every extracted data display shows a confidence level — **High / Medium / Low** — and flags:

- Missing Information
- Conflicting Information
- Incomplete Information
- Unverified Information

Review is required before accepting AI suggestions. ✅ built — every extracted field in the Upload Center shows High / Medium / Low confidence plus missing / conflicting / incomplete / unverified flags, with source document references.

### 2.4 Export acknowledgement (required gate)

Before any IEP, amendment, BIP, report, meeting summary, or parent summary can be downloaded or exported, the user must check all four:

- ☐ I understand this document contains AI-generated content.
- ☐ I have reviewed the content for accuracy and completeness.
- ☐ I understand all educational decisions remain the responsibility of qualified educational personnel.
- ☐ I understand this document may require further review prior to official use.

**User must acknowledge before export.** ✅ (built — a required acknowledgement dialog, `ExportGate`, gates every AI-assisted export: IEP drafts, source-citation reports, review reports, progress reports, caseload record binders, meeting packets, and the student IEP export.)

## 3. CORE PLATFORM — IEP STUDIO COMMAND CENTER ✅

IEP Studio is the **only** IEP entry point. The separate Documents page is removed; all document work happens inside the student's context. No secondary IEP workspaces.

**Screen flow (all on one screen, no navigation):**

```
Select Student → Upload Center → AI Analysis → Student Snapshot → Generate IEP
```

### 3.1 Upload Center ✅
- Renders **directly under the student selector** the instant a student is selected — never behind a tab.
- **FERPA "Protected Student Information" authorization notice displays directly above the Upload Center.**
- Drag & drop plus click-to-browse; supported: PDF, DOCX, JPG, PNG.
- Document type picker: IEP, MDT, Evaluation, Reevaluation, BIP, FBA, Progress Reports, Parent Input, Teacher Input, Speech/OT/PT reports, psychological, eligibility, medical, behavior log, discipline, assessment, 504, transition assessment, other.
- Files are stored **privately** (organization-only access via signed URLs).
- Duplicate filenames are blocked so AI credits are not re-spent.
- Upload is the mandatory first step of the workflow; there is no other upload point in the app.

### 3.2 Auto Document Analysis / OCR ✅
- On upload: queued → OCR/reading of every page → processing → processed/failed, with live status chips and retry on failure.
- Polls automatically while reading; no manual refresh required.

### 3.3 Auto-Fill Engine ✅
- After all documents are read, `autoBuildProfile` runs automatically (no extra clicks): extracts eligibility, strengths, areas of need, present levels, accommodations, SDI, services, goals, progress data, behavior information, and parent concerns.
- Returns per-field confidence levels, flags, and source references (§2.3).
- Pre-fills the student record; shows exactly which fields were pre-filled, kept as-is, and which data gaps remain. Educator review required.

### 3.4 Student Snapshot ✅
Displayed after analysis: eligibility, disability, strengths found, areas of need found, goals found, accommodations found, services found, behavior supports found, parent concerns found, documents reviewed, missing information.

### 3.5 IEP Readiness Score ✅
- Readiness card scores how meeting-ready the student's file is, with the missing items that would raise the score.

### 3.6 Documents on File ✅
- Read-only library inside IEP Studio: status chips, open (signed URL), retry failed, delete.

### 3.7 Student Dashboard ✅
- Overview tab: verified student record — demographics, IEP dates, compliance deadlines, strengths, needs, present levels, accommodations, services — plus Meeting Mode trigger and Copilot.

### 3.8 One-Click Actions ✅
Generate New IEP · Generate Amendment · Generate Meeting Script · Generate IEP Meeting Navigator · Generate Parent Summary · Generate Progress Summary · Review Compliance.

### 3.9 Student-context deep links ✅
- `/iep-studio?student=<id>` preselects the student; `/meetings?student=<id>` opens the schedule dialog with the student preselected.
- Every workflow link that crosses pages preserves student context — a teacher never reselects a student mid-workflow.
